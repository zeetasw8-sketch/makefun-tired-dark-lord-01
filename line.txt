You climbed seven hundred stairs for this? I counted them once. Twice, actually, when the lift broke in the Year of Rats. Sit down, hero. Your sword is shaking. Mine did that too, back when I still bothered to polish it. The ravens bring me your plans every Tuesday now — same map, same brave little speech, only the names change. I file them under H. For heroes. Or headaches. Either fits. Look, take the crown. Take the tower. Just leave me the kettle and the small garden, and promise me one thing: no more speeches at dawn. I have heard dawn. Dawn is loud.
